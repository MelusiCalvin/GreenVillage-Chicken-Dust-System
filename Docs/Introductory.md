# CHICKENHUB
## Business Management and Online Ordering System

### 1.1 Introduction

ChickenHub is a web-based business management and online ordering system designed for a small street-food business specialising in chicken dust (braaied chicken).

The system aims to improve the management of daily business operations by providing a centralised platform where the business owner and employees can manage inventory, record sales, monitor expenses, process customer orders and generate business reports.

In addition, ChickenHub will provide customers with an online ordering facility that allows them to view available products, place orders in advance and collect their food without having to follow the normal ordering process.

The system will be developed using Python and Django, with SQLite as the initial database management system and Bootstrap for the user interface.

### 1.2 Background

Small food businesses often manage their daily operations through manual processes. These may include recording sales, monitoring stock, calculating expenses and managing customer orders.

As the business grows, relying on manual processes can make it difficult to maintain accurate records, monitor stock levels and understand the overall financial performance of the business.

ChickenHub is proposed as a digital solution to support the business's operational activities while improving convenience for customers through online ordering.

### 1.3 Problem Statement

The business currently relies on physical books to record and store important operational information, including sales, stock and other business activities.

Although this approach allows the business to maintain daily records, it creates challenges when the owner needs to retrieve or review information from previous days, weeks or months. Finding specific historical records requires manually searching through old books, which can be time-consuming and inconvenient.

Additionally, some historical information has already been damaged, creating a risk of permanently losing valuable business records. This makes it difficult to consistently access accurate historical information and use past records to support business decisions.

ChickenHub aims to address this problem by introducing a centralised digital record-management system that securely stores business information and allows authorised users to retrieve historical records quickly. The system will also support inventory management, sales recording, expense tracking, reporting and online customer ordering.

### 1.4 Project Aim

The aim of ChickenHub is to develop and deploy a web-based system that simplifies business operations, improves record management and allows customers to place food orders online for collection.

### 1.5 Project Objectives

The objectives of ChickenHub are to:

1. Develop a secure system that allows authorised business users to access relevant features according to their roles.
2. Provide an inventory management module for recording stock purchases, quantities, stock movements and wastage.
3. Develop a sales management module for recording and tracking daily transactions.
4. Implement an expense management module to record business-related expenditure.
5. Provide reporting functionality to help the business monitor revenue, expenses, stock and estimated profit.
6. Develop an online ordering platform where customers can view products and place orders in advance.
7. Provide order management functionality to track orders from placement to collection.
8. Improve the efficiency of daily business operations and the customer ordering experience.

### 1.6 Project Scope

The initial version of ChickenHub will focus on the following areas:

**Business Management**
- User authentication and role-based access.
- Inventory and stock management.
- Sales recording and transaction management.
- Business expense tracking.
- Business dashboard and reports.
- Order processing and management.

**Customer Ordering**
- Online product catalogue.
- Product selection and shopping cart.
- Customer order placement.
- Collection order management.
- Order status tracking.

**Initial Technology Stack**
- Programming language: Python
- Backend framework: Django
- Database: SQLite
- Frontend framework: Bootstrap 5
- Frontend technologies: HTML5, CSS and JavaScript
- Version control: Git and GitHub

### 1.7 Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a functional and deployed web-based platform that enables the business to manage its daily activities more effectively while providing customers with a convenient way to order food in advance for collection.

### 1.8 Historical Data and Record Management

ChickenHub will introduce digital record-keeping from the date the system is implemented.

The business will not initially migrate its existing physical records into the system. Instead, all new business transactions and operational activities will be recorded digitally from the implementation date.

The system will maintain a centralised history of sales, inventory movements, expenses and customer orders. Authorised users will be able to retrieve previous records using dates, transaction details and relevant search filters.

This approach will gradually reduce the business's dependence on physical record books while ensuring that new business information is stored in an organised and accessible format.
